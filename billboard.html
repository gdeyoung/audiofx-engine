AUDIO FX ENGINE / GDEYOUNG

A filter-chain rack
for PipeWire.

One virtual sink, four convolver banks — HeSuVi HRIR 3D, bass, night-mode and movie EQ — applied transactionally from a single CLI. Ships as systemd user units that self-install on first apply; a watch router re-pins streams as devices come and go.
  • 3D via HeSuVi HRIR convolution
  • Bass & night IR banks, freely mixed
  • Movie EQ preset, three bands
  • Transactional apply — no half-built graphs
  • Per-app bypass rules
  • Self-installing systemd user units
  • Watch router relinks on hotplug
  • One CLI: status, apply, rules
